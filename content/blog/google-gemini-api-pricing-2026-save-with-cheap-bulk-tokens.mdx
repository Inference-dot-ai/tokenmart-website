## What is Google Gemini API Pricing?

**Google gemini api pricing** is defined as the usage-based financial structure that Google imposes on developers for integrating its Gemini Large Language Models (LLMs) into external applications. In 2026, that structure remains strictly token-based. Developers are billed in increments of one million tokens for both their input prompts and the model's generated outputs.

The complexity stems from the sheer variety of available models. Each generation and tier carries a completely different cost profile. Consequently, evaluating your options requires a deep understanding of your application's specific technical requirements. Audio, visual, and text inputs all consume tokens at distinct, varying rates. 

Furthermore, unexpected features can dynamically alter your final bill. For example, the hidden cost of "thinking tokens" during advanced reasoning tasks can inflate output billing unexpectedly. This intricate billing environment highlights exactly why managing standard retail rates often overwhelms fast-growing software companies.

### The Mechanics of Token Billing

To truly master these costs, you must understand the dichotomy between input and output tokens. Input tokens represent the raw data, instructions, and context you feed into the model. Output tokens represent the active textual or multimodal generation the model produces in response. 

Because generation requires significantly more server compute power, output costs are universally higher. On the standard rate card, output tokens can cost up to six times more than input tokens. Additionally, new reasoning models utilize "thinking tokens" to solve complex problems behind the scenes. Under standard billing rules, these internal thinking tokens count as billed output tokens, which rapidly accelerates your costs.

### Current Gemini Model Tiers in 2026

The precise rate you pay depends entirely on your selected model tier. Google restructured its lineup heavily in 2026, offering diverse options for different commercial workloads. 

For example, Gemini 3.1 Pro is the flagship reasoning model. It lists at $2.00 per million input tokens and $12.00 per million output tokens for contexts under 200,000 tokens. If you exceed that context window, the long-context rate rises to $4.00 and $18.00, respectively.

Conversely, lighter models offer financial relief. Gemini 3.5 Flash launched with a highly competitive **google gemini api pricing** of $1.50 for input and $9.00 for output per million tokens. For extreme budget constraints, Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite drops to just $0.10 for input and $0.40 for output.
