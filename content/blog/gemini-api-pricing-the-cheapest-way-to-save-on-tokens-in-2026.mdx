### Input Tokens vs. Output Tokens
It all comes back to input and output tokens, because these represent the fundamental units of AI data processing. An **input token** is a piece of text, image, or audio you send to the model. An **output token** is the response the AI generates and sends back to you. Traditionally, input tokens are significantly cheaper than output tokens. For instance, analyzing a 100-page document costs far less than having the AI write a 10-page original report.

### The New Complexities of Billing
However, the latest billing structure introduces critical new complexities. High-tier reasoning models now utilize internal "thinking tokens". The AI generates these tokens internally to process complex logical workflows before returning a final answer. The hidden catch is that these internal thinking tokens are billed at the much higher output token rate. Consequently, the advertised baseline rate on standard developer pages rarely reflects the actual invoice you receive at the end of the month. 

Your effective rate also fluctuates heavily based on context window utilization. Feeding hundreds of thousands of tokens per prompt drives up costs rapidly. Navigating these multi-layered tiers natively requires constant vigilance.
